Design language · v1

Private by default.
Quiet by design.

Lowki is an embedded wallet that keeps your keys on your device and your apps unaware of each other. The look follows the name: low-key, calm, and green like a lauki.

The mark

A lauki in low-key light

In photography, low-key means mostly shadow and a single light. The mark is a lauki in the dark: one lime rim of light along its edge, and light coming through a keyhole. On dark surfaces only the light remains, and the eye completes the gourd.

Lauki

Bottle gourd: smooth, round and long, swelling gently into its belly. Where "lowki" comes from.

Low-key light

Almost everything in shadow. Only a rim of light and a keyhole glow. Privacy as a mood.

Lowki

Both together: a lauki in shadow, one edge of lime light, and light through its keyhole.

Logo set

Marks and lockups

Clear space equals the bulb radius. Use the solid mark on light surfaces and the night mark on dark ones. Below 32 px use the app icon: an ink lauki on a lime tile. The wordmark is always lowercase.

Colour

Lime to act, lauki to rest

Lime is a fill for the one thing to do next. It is never text on light surfaces. Lauki and gourd-flesh tints carry the brand quietly. Anything private sits on ink.

Type

Three faces, bundled

Bricolage Grotesque for titles and balances, Instrument Sans for everything you read, IBM Plex Mono for addresses and amounts. All SIL OFL, shipped with the app so no font request ever leaves the page.
