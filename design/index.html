Design language · v1

Private by default.
Quiet by design.

Lowki is an embedded wallet that keeps your keys on your device and your apps unaware of each other. The look follows the name: low-key, calm, and green like a lauki.

The mark

A gourd that locks

A club-shaped lauki is a keyhole turned upside down. The mark is a lime gourd with a keyhole cut into its body, so it reads as the vegetable and as a lock in the same glance.

Lauki

Bottle gourd. Long neck, round body. Where "lowki" comes from.

Keyhole

Turn the same silhouette over and it's a keyhole. Privacy, without saying it.

Lowki

Both at once: a lime gourd with a keyhole cut through it, and a lauki leaf.

Logo set

Marks and lockups

Clear space equals twice the keyhole circle. The mark must be at least 16 px tall; below 24 px use the app icon. The wordmark is always lowercase, and the dot on its i is a tiny gourd.

Colour

Lime to act, lauki to rest

Lime is a fill for the one thing to do next. It is never text on light surfaces. Lauki and gourd-flesh tints carry the brand quietly. Anything private sits on ink.

Type

Three faces, bundled

Bricolage Grotesque for titles and balances, Instrument Sans for everything you read, IBM Plex Mono for addresses and amounts. All SIL OFL, shipped with the app so no font request ever leaves the page.
